HandyConnect Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your HandyConnect account and personal information at any time.

How to request deletion

Send a WhatsApp message to +27 71 289 0446 stating that you want your HandyConnect account and personal data deleted. Please send the request from the same phone number associated with your HandyConnect account so we can verify the request.

What happens next

We will verify the request, delete or de-identify personal information that is no longer required, and confirm completion through the same contact channel. Some records may be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

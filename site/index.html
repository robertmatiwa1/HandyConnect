Local services, connected through WhatsApp.

HandyConnect is a WhatsApp-first marketplace that helps customers request trusted local services and gives verified handymen a simple way to discover and manage genuine work opportunities.

What the product does

The primary product experience runs inside WhatsApp. Customers can browse services before registering, describe a job naturally, provide a location and optional photo, then track the request. Service providers manage availability, opportunities and jobs from the same conversational interface.

Customer marketplace

Request plumbing, electrical, roofing, appliance repair and other home/trade services with a guided WhatsApp journey.

Provider marketplace

Verified service providers set skills, service areas and availability, then receive ranked opportunities relevant to them.

Trust & control

Verification state, job history, ratings, reporting, audit events and first-accept-wins assignment support a safer marketplace.

Customer journey

No separate app download or website login is required for the core customer journey.

Service-provider journey

Providers register their trade profile and operating area, control when they are available, and only see opportunities that fit the marketplace matching rules.

OnboardProvider profile captures identity/contact details, skill categories, base area and service coverage.
Set availabilityProvider chooses an availability window; expired availability automatically removes them from active matching.
Receive opportunitiesMatching considers skill, location coverage, availability, access plan, verification, rating and job history.
Accept & completeAtomic first-accept-wins assignment prevents two providers from taking the same job; job lifecycle is recorded through completion and rating.

Pricing model

HandyConnect charges service providers for access to genuine marketplace opportunities. Customers are not charged to browse services.

Free provider access
R0 / month

Up to 3 marketplace opportunities per month.

Recurring provider subscription for expanded marketplace access, processed through Paystack hosted checkout.

Customer browsing
R0

Customers can browse supported services without paying or registering. Registration is required when submitting a service request.

Payments & data

The current Paystack integration is for provider subscription billing. HandyConnect does not operate a customer wallet or independently hold customer funds.

AreaCurrent implementation
Payment purposeHandyConnect Pro provider subscription (R99/month).
CheckoutPaystack hosted checkout; browser callbacks do not grant access.
EntitlementOnly a provider-confirmed successful payment creates or extends Pro access.
Payment securitySigned Paystack webhook handling with event deduplication; payment records and subscription state are persisted.
Customer fundsNo HandyConnect customer wallet or stored-value account is operated.
Personal dataData minimisation is applied. Customer and provider information is collected only for marketplace, fulfilment, trust and billing purposes.

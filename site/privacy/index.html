HandyConnect Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 August 2026

HandyConnect is a WhatsApp-first marketplace that connects customers who need home repair, maintenance and improvement services with local handymen and skilled service providers. This Privacy Policy explains how HandyConnect collects, uses, shares and protects personal information.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your name, mobile number, email address, business name, skills, service areas, job descriptions, job locations, conversation messages, job activity, ratings and reviews, verification information, subscription status and payment records. Payment card details are processed by our payment service provider and are not stored by HandyConnect.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to create and manage accounts, match customers with suitable service providers, send service-related WhatsApp messages, manage subscriptions and lead access, support verification and trust features, prevent fraud and abuse, provide customer support, improve matching and service quality, and comply with legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp and service providers

HandyConnect uses the WhatsApp Business Platform as a primary communication channel. We also use technology and payment service providers, including infrastructure and database providers, to operate the service. We only share information that is reasonably necessary for those providers to perform their services.

4. Customer and handyman information sharing

When a customer requests a service, relevant job information may be shared with suitable registered handymen for matching purposes. Contact or assignment information may be shared where necessary to enable the selected customer and handyman to communicate and complete the job.

5. Payments

HandyConnect may charge handymen for subscriptions, verification, lead credits or related platform services. Payment transactions are processed by third-party payment providers. HandyConnect records transaction status and references but does not store full payment card details.

6. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and service records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or ask questions about how your information is handled.

9. Account and data deletion

You may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting HandyConnect using the contact details published by the service. Certain records may be retained where required by law or where necessary for legitimate fraud-prevention, dispute or accounting purposes.

10. Children's privacy

HandyConnect is intended for adults who can lawfully request or provide services. We do not knowingly target children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will remain available on this page and will show its effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the HandyConnect operator through the official HandyConnect contact channel or the contact email associated with the HandyConnect service.
